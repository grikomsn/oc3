# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, constructive, and mindful that contributors have different backgrounds and experience levels.

Acceptable participation includes:

- Discussing ideas and disagreements without attacking people
- Giving actionable feedback and accepting corrections gracefully
- Keeping discussions on the project's scope

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, and publishing others' private information. Maintainers may remove comments, issues, or pull requests that violate this policy.

## Reporting

Report conduct issues to [griko@nibras.co](mailto:griko@nibras.co). Reports are handled confidentially.
